Prolific Study Alerts

What we do

Prolific Study Alerts is a Chrome extension that monitors app.prolific.com/studies for newly available paid studies and sends you instant notifications via Telegram.

Data we collect

🔒 We do not store any personal data, study data, or browsing history.

Study information (title, reward, link) is forwarded to your Telegram in real time and is not stored on our servers. It is sent once and discarded.

How it works

The extension runs a content script on Prolific's study page. When a new study card appears in the page, the extension sends a Telegram message to you. That's it.

Prolific Study Alerts is currently free to use.
